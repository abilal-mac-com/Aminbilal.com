ONE-LINE-STANDFIRST

Amin Bilal in collaboration with CLAUDE-MODEL

OPENING-PARAGRAPH. Set the scene in the first two sentences and give the reader the reason to continue in the third. The house voice is first person, unhurried, and concrete before it is abstract.

MIDDLE-PARAGRAPH. One idea per paragraph. Figures precise and verified on the day of writing; where a figure cannot be sourced, leave it out rather than round it into something that reads well.

MIDDLE-PARAGRAPH. Add as many of these as the argument needs. Em-dashes as —, apostrophes as ’, quotation marks as “ and ”.

CLOSING-PARAGRAPH. Land the point rather than summarising it. A short final sentence usually does more work than a long one.
